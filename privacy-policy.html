Privacy Policy

How Barrow Plumbing & Heating handles and protects your personal information.

Barrow Plumbing & Heating ("we", "us", or "our"), located at 2 Garnett St, Huntingdale VIC 3166, is committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our customers, website visitors, and enquirers in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) set out in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).
