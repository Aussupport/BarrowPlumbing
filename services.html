Everyday Maintenance

General Plumbing & Leak Repairs

Water leaks can quickly cause extensive damage to flooring, plaster, and building frameworks if left unresolved. We provide thorough diagnostic testing and permanent repairs.

Whether it is an irritating dripping shower spindle, a continuously trickling toilet cistern wasting hundreds of litres of water, or noisy water hammer in your pipes, we carry quality replacement components to resolve the issue promptly.

  • Dripping tapware, mixer cartridge replacements & reseating
  • Toilet leak diagnostics, inlet valves & rubber seal renewals
  • Burst pipe location and copper / PEX pipe repairs
  • Water pressure assessment & pressure reduction valves

Hot Water Specialists

Hot Water System Servicing & Replacements

Nothing disrupts a morning routine faster than stepping into an icy shower. Barrow Plumbing & Heating provides fast, reliable diagnostic and repair services for domestic hot water systems.

We inspect gas storage units, continuous flow systems, and electric hot water cylinders. If your unit is leaking water from the tank, blowing safety valves, or failing to heat up, we provide practical advice on whether a targeted repair or unit replacement is the most cost-effective solution.

  • Cold water and lukewarm water temperature troubleshooting
  • Replacement of temperature & pressure relief (TPR) valves
  • Thermostat & thermocouple diagnostics
  • Hot water unit replacement and installation advice

Winter Comfort & Safety

Heating Maintenance & Servicing

Melbourne winters demand dependable home heating. Regular maintenance ensures your heating appliances operate at optimal efficiency and, most importantly, safely.

We service domestic heating units, check heat exchangers, inspect burners, and assess gas line integrity. Ensuring proper ventilation and clean burning is vital for safeguarding your household against carbon monoxide hazards.

  • Seasonal heater servicing and pre-winter tune-ups
  • Burner cleaning, dust removal & fan maintenance
  • Pilot light, spark ignition, and flame sensor repairs
  • Heating safety inspections & airflow checks

Drainage Solutions

Blocked Drains & Sewer Repairs

Slow draining sinks, gurgling toilets, or water bubbling up from outside overflow gullies indicate a severe restriction in your drainage or sewer lines.

Blockages are often triggered by grease accumulation, foreign objects, collapsed pipes, or tree root intrusion common in mature Melbourne suburbs. We quickly pinpoint the blockage location and clear it safely without damaging your pipes.

  • Unblocking blocked kitchen sinks, baths, and laundry troughs
  • Overflowing sewer lines and external drain clearing
  • Tree root clearing and drain maintenance
  • Stormwater downpipe clearing & pit maintenance

Safety First

Gas Fitting & Leak Detection

Gas work requires uncompromising safety standards, precision, and adherence to Australian regulatory codes. We take gas safety with utmost seriousness.

If you ever notice a rotten-egg gas smell, hear hissing near an appliance, or notice unusually high gas bills, contact us immediately. We carry electronic gas leak detectors and manometer pressure testing equipment to isolate and repair leaks safely.

  • Emergency gas leak isolation & pipe repair
  • Gas cooktop, oven & stove installation
  • Gas bayonet point installation for heaters and barbecues
  • Gas pressure testing and compliance checks
